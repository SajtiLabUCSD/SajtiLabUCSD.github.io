**About the PI**  
[Eniko Sajti](https://profiles.ucsd.edu/eniko.sajti) is an associate professor at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/) and a board-certified pediatrician and neonatologist at [Rady Children's Hospital](https://www.rchsd.org/). She received her medical degree and PhD from [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/en) in the Netherlands. As an awardee of the Dutch Royal Academy of Sciences she completed a postdoctoral fellowship in pulmonary medicine at [UC San Francisco](https://www.ucsf.edu/). After completing her pediatric residency and neonatology fellowship at [Harvard Medical School/Boston Children's Hospital](https://hms.harvard.edu/departments/hms-celiac-research-program/people/boston-childrens-hospital), she was recruited to UC San Diego Department of Pediatrics.

**Our focus - [more details](https://sajtilabucsd.github.io/research/)**  
Myeloid cell function and the innate immune system  
Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD)  
Lung macrophage and monocyte subtypes  
Genetic variation in susceptibility to lung disease
